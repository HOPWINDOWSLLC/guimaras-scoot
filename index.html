FAQ

Do I need a driver's license?

Yes. Bring a valid license (Philippine or foreign) and one ID. You can upload a photo in the waiver step to speed up pickup.

How much is it?

A flat $3 per hour or $14 per day (about ₱/hr or ₱/day). If you rent for more than 4 hours, the daily rate works out cheaper, and the form suggests it automatically.

Can you meet my ferry?

Yes. Choose "ferry arrival pickup" and the approximate time. Pump boats from Iloilo City (Ortiz or Parola) take about 15 minutes to Jordan Wharf.

Where can I get fuel?

There are gas stations around Jordan and San Miguel. Fill up before riding south to Nueva Valencia and Guisi, because stations get sparse. Roadside "bottled" gasoline is common in barangays.

What if it rains?

Add a raincoat (+$0.50). Rainy season is roughly June to November. Ride slowly on wet, hilly roads.

Can I extend my rental? Is late return a full day?

Yes, you can extend with one tap under "Extend" in My bookings, at the same rate: $3 per hour, up to $14 per day. No "5 hours late = full day" rule.

Do you keep my passport?

No. We use a refundable GCash/Maya hold (or cash) that is returned at drop-off.

I arrive on the first or last boat. Can you meet me?

Yes. Ferry pickup runs from about 04:00 to 19:00. Choose an early or late slot when booking.
